Rule-of-thumb estimate only: assumes a 30-year loan and an 8.5% serviceability assessment rate. The income safety scale is based on loan-to-income multiple: under 6x is comfortably within what most lenders will approve, 6x-8x is tight and depends heavily on your expenses, debts and the specific lender, and above 8x is unlikely to be approved by most mainstream lenders. Deposit required assumes a 20% deposit to avoid lenders mortgage insurance (LMI); the ready-by date is calculated from today's date at your chosen monthly savings rate and assumes that rate stays constant. Post-tax income now applies simplified Australian resident tax brackets plus the 2% Medicare levy to each person's income separately and sums the result, so it's a closer match to real take-home pay than a single combined-income estimate. The deposit slider is capped at the property value. Real lenders also weigh living expenses, other debts, dependents and credit history. This is not financial or lending advice.
